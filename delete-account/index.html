Delete Your Account - Debt Counter

Developer: Debt Counter App

If you want to delete your account and associated data, follow the steps below:

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to: your-email@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject: "Delete my Debt Counter account"
  3. Include the email used in the app

What data will be deleted

  • User account information (email, authentication data)
  • Expense and group data stored in Firebase

Data that may be retained

Some data may be retained for legal, security, or fraud prevention purposes for up to 30 days.

Processing time

Deletion requests are processed within 7–14 days.
